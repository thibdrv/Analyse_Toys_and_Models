# Toys and Models

### Project Description :
Designed and implemented a dynamic executive dashboard for a model and scale-model retail company to streamline daily decision-making across core operations. Conducted exploratory data analysis (EDA) on an existing relational database housing employees, products, sales orders, and logistical records. Transformed complex multi-domain data into an automated, single-pane management tool refreshed daily for executive oversight.

The project is divided into four parts :

1. Human Resources : Sales team performance

2. Sales : Product trends and margins

3. Finance : Payments and cash flow

4. Supply Chain : Inventory and delivery speed

### KPI :

Human Resources :

- Sales Representative Performance: Calculate the revenue generated by each sales representative.
- Orders-to-Payments Ratio by Sales Rep: Identify gaps between placed orders and received payments for each representative.
- Office Performance: Measure the revenue generated by each office location.

Sales :

- Monthly Revenue & Growth Rate by Region: Track revenue by region and month alongside month-over-month growth rates to identify geographic trends.
- Top & Bottom Performing Products by Category: Identify the best and worst-selling products within each category.
- Gross Margin by Product & Category: Measure gross margin to determine the most and least profitable products and categories.
- Monthly Sales Growth Rate by Category: Track the performance of each product category over time.
- Average Order Value (AOV): Calculate the average total value per customer order.
- Customer Retention Rate: Measure customer loyalty by identifying repeat buyers.

Finance :

- Top & Bottom Revenue-Generating Customers: Identify high-value customers to improve retention strategies.
- Accounts Receivable Collection Rate by Customer: Pinpoint customers with high outstanding unpaid balances.
- Quarterly Sales Growth: Identify seasonal trends and macro growth opportunities.
- Average Payment Amount & Below-Average Customers: Evaluate customer payment capacity and baseline transaction size.
- Payment Aging / Lead Time: Measure average payment turnaround time following order placement.

Supply Chain :

- Low Stock & Reorder Alerts: Identify products falling below critical inventory thresholds to prevent stockouts.
- Average Order Fulfillment Time & Outlier Delays: Assess operational efficiency by measuring the time from order placement to dispatch, highlighting orders exceeding average fulfillment times.
- Inventory Turnover Rate: Measure how quickly inventory is sold and replaced for each product.
- Late Delivery Rate: Identify supply chain bottlenecks and improve overall delivery performance.
